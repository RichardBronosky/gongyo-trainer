## Current Rule

- Suspect a cell when its romanization contains two vowel-bearing units.
- Treat a consonant-plus-`y` opening as one unit: `kyo`, `myo`, `nyo`, `ryo`, and similar cells are not candidates solely because of `y`.
- Do not infer a second spoken syllable from a final consonant in contracted spellings such as `bus`, `jip`, `sep`, or `sok`; adjacent cells already carry the following sound.
- Treat the confirmed `-ai` forms above as one spoken syllable.
